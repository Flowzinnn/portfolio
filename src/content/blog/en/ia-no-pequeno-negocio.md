Everyone is talking about artificial intelligence. The problem is that many companies start in the wrong place: they subscribe to a trendy tool, nobody uses it properly, and the money is gone.

Running technology in an environment where nothing can stop taught me that the safe path always goes through three questions.

## 1. Where does your team lose the most time?

Don't start with the technology. Start with the routine. Answering the same questions on WhatsApp, copying data from one spreadsheet to another, hunting for a contract buried in email… If it happens every day and follows a pattern, it is a strong candidate for automation.

## 2. What happens if it goes wrong?

AI makes mistakes. The right question is: how big is the damage? Start with tasks where an error is easy to spot and fix — and always keep a person reviewing anything sensitive.

## 3. Is your data ready?

AI is only as good as the information it receives. If everything is scattered across chats and paper, the first step is to organize the basics. It's cheap, and it completely changes the outcome.

## The ideal first project

- Happens many times a week
- Follows a clear pattern
- Is low-risk if it goes wrong
- Lets you measure before and after

Found a task like that? That's your starting point. Start small, measure the result, and only then expand.
